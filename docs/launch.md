# Sharing Twinquiry

Start with a reproducible use case rather than a broad claim that two models are always better than one. Show the question, initial differences, one useful objection, and the resulting change. A short terminal recording plus a readable report is enough.

Suggested sequence:

1. **GitHub:** a working quick start, a clearly marked offline demo, current limitations, and issue templates or specific feedback questions.
2. **X:** a short demo thread. Link the repository and explain one concrete difference revealed by cross-review.
3. **Reddit:** relevant Claude Code and Codex communities, after checking their current self-promotion rules. Share the method and findings; ask a focused technical question.
4. **Show HN:** publish when readers can actually try the tool. Stay available to answer implementation and methodology questions. [Official guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html).
5. **DEV.to or Hashnode:** a tutorial covering setup, usage, failure recovery, and interpreting disagreement.
6. **Product Hunt:** consider a broader launch after early feedback improves onboarding. [Launch guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch).

A possible title: **Twinquiry: independent Claude and Codex research with a cross-review trail**.

Do not present synthetic demo outputs as live model evidence, or a small case study as a general benchmark. Publish measured results only with the source packet, model identities where known, relevant settings, and limitations. Avoid claims of perfect consensus or guaranteed savings.
