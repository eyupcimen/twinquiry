# Research question

Write the decision or question in one sentence.

## Why this matters

What will you do with the result?

## Known constraints

List confirmed requirements, budget, timing, platform, or scope limits.

## Unknown preferences

List decisions you have not made. Ask the models to explain the options and keep recommendations conditional.

## Evidence

Use --source to attach UTF-8 documents containing relevant excerpts, original URLs, and retrieval dates.
If you need fresh web research, choose --web and specify the date range and preferred source types.

## Expected result

Ask for alternatives, supporting evidence, counterarguments, open questions, and practical next steps.
